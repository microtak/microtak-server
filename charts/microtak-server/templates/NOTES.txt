Enroll a device to get started (see the main repo's README "Building and
running" for what enrollment actually does):
  openssl req -new -newkey rsa:2048 -nodes -keyout device.key -subj "/CN=my-device" -out device.csr
  curl -X POST http://<enrollment-address>:{{ .Values.service.ports.enrollment }}/Marti/api/tls/signClient/v2 \
    -H "Content-Type: application/octet-stream" --data-binary @device.csr

every previously-issued client certificate). Fine for a quick trial, not
for anything real.
